⚙️ Settings
Change how the app works — no coding. Each box saves on its own; customers see the change on their next page open.
🧾
Orders & paymentsPause new orders when you are busy, and choose how customers pay
Pause orders (busy mode)
…
While paused, customers can still look around but can't send orders. Home page, cart and checkout show your message.
Payment methods at checkout
Ticked = customers can choose it. At least one must stay on.
Visitors from outside Saudi Arabia
Only website visitors whose internet is outside Saudi Arabia. Never: customers in Saudi Arabia, our iPhone / Android app, Google, or anyone who already ordered on that phone. If the country can't be found, the visitor is let in.
Customers can cancel their order
Only while the order is still waiting and no rider is assigned. After that the customer sees your WhatsApp instead. You hear the alarm and the order shows "❌ By customer" with the reason.

Supermarket orders
Minimum and maximum count only the supermarket items in the cart. Below the minimum the customer sees "add X SAR more"; above the maximum: "contact us on WhatsApp".

Photo speed — سرعة الصور
Checking your photos…
Makes your shop, dish and product photos smaller so they open fast, and lets phones keep them for 1 year instead of 1 hour. Your original photos are kept — Undo puts them back. New photos you upload are made small automatically. Best done once from Chrome on a computer; keep this page open while it works.
🎁 Loyalty reminders
Automatic push to app customers: one order away from the free delivery (9 of 10), just earned it (10 of 10), or not used after 7 days. Max 1 reminder a week per customer, only between 12 PM and 9 PM.
